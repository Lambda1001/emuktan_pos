Emuktan Interface Design

A working mockup of the dispensary app in the Apple HIG style, adapted for Windows. Try signing in and making a sale. All names, stock and prices here are samples.

Window drawn at 1040 px minimum width. Scroll sideways on a narrow screen.

Principles

Apple's guidelines are about how an interface feels to use. These are the seven that decide most choices in Emuktan.

Clarity

One job per screen. Totals and stock are the largest things on Sell. Nothing is set below 12 px.

Deference

White panels on a faint grey window, thin separators. Colour is kept for meaning, so a warning is always noticed.

Depth

Only sheets float above the page. Everything else is flat and quiet.

Consistency

Buttons, fields, chips and tables look the same on every screen, and come from tokens.

Direct manipulation

Click a medicine to add it. Change quantity and unit in the row. No separate edit pages.

Feedback

Every action shows a result: a toast, a changed state, or a sheet. Nothing fails silently.

User control

Confirm only what is destructive or hard to undo. Offline is normal, not an error.

Colour

One accent, a clinical blue. Success, warning and critical are separate from it and always come with an icon or a word. Every text pair passes 4.5:1 in both themes. Values below follow the theme you are viewing.

Type

Segoe UI Variable on Windows 11, with Segoe UI as fallback. The app never downloads fonts, because it has to work with no internet. Figures are tabular so money lines up.

Space and shape

A 4 pt grid. Controls are 40 px high, and 48 px on the Sell screen where speed matters. Panels use a 12 px radius, controls 8 px, sheets 16 px.

Rules for behaviour

  • Scanner firstSearch takes focus when Sell opens. A scan plus Enter adds the item. Ctrl+K jumps to search. Esc closes a sheet.
  • Units are explicitEach medicine shows only the ways it is sold. The row states what leaves stock, for example "2 strips = 20 tablets".
  • Batches are automaticThe batch that expires first is used and shown. Staff do not pick batches during a sale.
  • Discounts need a clinicianA cashier cannot apply one alone. A clinician enters their PIN, and the approver is recorded on the sale.
  • The button says what happens"Charge KES 1,320", not "Submit". When it is disabled, a line under it says why.
  • Offline is normalNo error dialogs for a lost connection. The status bar says it, and sales continue. A backup older than 24 hours turns the bar amber.
  • Plain warnings"Expired. Do not sell." beats a code. Quantities always carry the unit.
